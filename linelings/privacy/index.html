Linelings (排排Go) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Developer: BT Studio

What the game stores

Your progress, settings, coins and purchases are saved only on your device. We do not run servers, accounts or chat, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players who have not bought “Remove Ads” see ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. If you decline tracking, only non-personalized ads are shown. In the EEA, UK and other regions you can choose your consent in the privacy message shown at first launch. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Friend challenges

Challenge codes you share contain only the level, your time, hints used and the nickname you typed. They are shared only when you choose to send them.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can turn off ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, buy “Remove Ads”, or delete the app to erase all data. Settings › Reset all data also clears progress.

隱私權政策（繁體中文）

遊戲進度、設定、點數與購買紀錄只存在你的裝置上；我們沒有伺服器、帳號或聊天功能，也不收集你的姓名、電子郵件、聯絡人或位置。付款由 Apple 處理，我們看不到付款資料。未購買「去廣告」的玩家會看到 Google AdMob 廣告；Google 可能收集廣告識別碼（僅在你允許追蹤時）、由 IP 推估的大概位置與廣告互動資訊，用來顯示與衡量廣告；拒絕追蹤時只顯示非個人化廣告。好友挑戰碼只包含關卡、時間、提示次數與你輸入的暱稱，只有你主動分享時才會送出。本遊戲為一般向益智遊戲，並非針對 13 歲以下兒童。聯絡我們：bennytsengstudio@gmail.com。
